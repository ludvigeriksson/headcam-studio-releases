# Headcam Studio

Turn headcam footage into a video with your route, map and stats on top.

Headcam Studio combines a video from your head-mounted camera with the GPS recording from your watch, and for orienteering a picture of the map. The overlays follow the video: your position moving across the map, pace, distance, heart rate, elevation and split times.

Built for orienteering, and works for running, cycling, skiing, hiking and other sports too. Everything stays on your computer; nothing is uploaded.

## Download

**[Get the latest version](https://github.com/ludvigeriksson/headcam-studio-releases/releases/latest)** and pick the file for your computer:

| Computer | File ending in |
| --- | --- |
| Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or later) | `mac-arm64.dmg` |
| Mac with an Intel processor | `mac-x64.dmg` |
| Windows | `win-x64.exe` |

The other files on the release page are used by the app to update itself; you don't need them.

## Install

**Mac:** open the `.dmg` and drag Headcam Studio to Applications. Start it from there, not from the disk image.

**Windows:** run the `.exe`. Windows may show a "Windows protected your PC" warning; choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

## Updates

The app updates itself. When a new version has been downloaded, it shows **Restart to update**; otherwise the update is installed the next time you quit.

## What you need

- A video from your camera (MP4 or MOV)
- A GPS recording of the same activity (FIT, GPX or TCX)
- For orienteering: an image of the map (PNG or JPEG)
